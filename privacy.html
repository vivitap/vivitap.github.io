Vivitap Privacy

Vivitap is dedicated to highly private solutions.

Connection Tracker (Contrac)

Updated September 13, 2026. This section describes the Connection Tracker Windows app.

Internet connectivity checks

While running, the app sends ICMP echo requests (pings) to Cloudflare (1.1.1.2), Google (8.8.8.8), Cisco OpenDNS (208.67.222.222), and Quad9 (9.9.9.9) to measure response times and detect connection outages. These services and network operators can observe the requests and their source IP address. Their own policies govern their handling of that network information.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps connectivity samples in memory and stores graph settings in your Windows user registry. It automatically saves outage logs in your Documents folder and can save a detailed sample report when you select the report command. A detailed report is written to the app's working directory. Log filenames include your computer name and the session's start time. Depending on the report, contents include destination IP addresses, timestamps, response times, and outage durations.

The app does not upload these logs to Vivitap or the connectivity-check providers. It does not include advertising or app analytics reporting to Vivitap.

Sharing, backup, and deletion

Review logs before sharing them with an internet service provider or anyone else: their filenames can identify your computer. Windows, OneDrive, or other backup software may copy saved files according to your settings. That backup or sharing is controlled by you and those services, not by a log-upload feature in the app.

Close the app to stop its connectivity checks and discard in-memory samples. Saved logs remain until you delete them; uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove files in Documents or their backup copies. Manage those copies separately.
